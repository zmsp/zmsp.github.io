Phrasebooks teach scripted questions like *"¿Dónde está el baño?"*, but fail the moment a native speaker responds at normal speed. 

Real fluency relies on two things: **high-frequency vocabulary** and **spaced repetition**. Here is how to apply both immediately.

---

## 1. Frequency First: The 80/20 Rule

Languages follow a power law. Instead of memorizing random vocabulary themes (colors, zoo animals, obscure kitchen utensils), prioritize word families by actual usage:

- **Top 1,000 words:** cover ~80% of everyday spoken Spanish.
- **Top 2,000 words:** cover ~97% of daily conversation.
{: .notice--info}

Once you know the top 2,000 words, you can infer unknown words from context and carry natural conversations without stalling.

---

## 2. Immediate Speaking Hacks

You do not need full verb conjugation tables to start communicating clearly.

### The Modal Anchor Hack
Combine high-frequency anchor verbs with any infinitive:

| Anchor | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Quiero** | I want | *Quiero pagar.* (I want to pay.) |
| **Necesito** | I need | *Necesito encontrar mi hotel.* (I need to find my hotel.) |
| **Voy a** | I'm going to | *Voy a comer.* (I'm going to eat.) |

**Formula:** `[Quiero / Necesito / Voy a] + [Infinitive]` expresses almost any intent without conjugating.
{: .notice--info}

### Instant Cognates
English and Spanish share thousands of Latin roots. Convert English words into Spanish with these reliable patterns:

- **-tion** &rarr; **-ción**: *information* &rarr; *información*, *action* &rarr; *acción*
- **-ty** &rarr; **-dad**: *city* &rarr; *ciudad*, *reality* &rarr; *realidad*
- **-ous** &rarr; **-oso/a**: *delicious* &rarr; *delicioso*, *curious* &rarr; *curioso*

---

## 3. Retain Vocabulary with Spaced Repetition

Rote repetition does not stick. Hermann Ebbinghaus's forgetting curve shows humans forget roughly 50% of new information within 24 hours, and up to 90% within a week.

**Spaced Repetition (SRS)** solves this:
- Algorithmic schedulers (like SM-2 or FSRS) calculate when memory stability is about to drop.
- Easy words get reviewed weeks later; difficult words reappear sooner.
- 10&ndash;15 minutes daily yields higher retention than multi-hour weekend cram sessions.
