I wanted to get conversational in Spanish without spending months on beginner filler. The thing is, the top 1000 most common words cover around 80% of everyday conversation, so if you just learn those first you get useful fast.

**TopWords** is a flashcard app built around that idea. Words are sorted by real-world frequency, the SM-2 algorithm schedules your reviews so you don't waste time on stuff you already know, and it works fully offline.

{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/projects/topwords-header.webp" alt="TopWords Banner" popup=true %}

## Key Features

{% include feature_row %}

* **Native Audio & Pronunciation**: High-clarity speech synthesis for every single vocabulary term and contextual example sentence.
* **Daily Goals & Habit Streaks**: Set custom daily review targets, view session performance breakdowns, and build lasting consistency with streak milestones.
* **100% Offline & Private**: All vocabulary banks, spaced repetition schedules, and user statistics live on-device with zero account sign-up requirements or internet dependencies.
* **Free & Distraction-Free**: No subscriptions, no ads, and no locked decks.

## Tech Stack

* **Framework**: Flutter & Riverpod
* **Algorithms**: SuperMemo-2 (SM-2) Spaced Repetition Engine
* **Storage**: Local SQLite via `sqflite` (with FFI web support)
* **Audio**: Flutter TTS (Text-to-Speech)
* **Platforms**: iOS, Android, and Web (Cloudflare Pages)
